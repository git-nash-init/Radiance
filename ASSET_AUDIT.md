# Asset audit — RADIANCE / Adinarayan Buildcon LLP

Every asset the client supplied, what it is, and where it's used on the site. Web versions are generated by the scripts in `scripts/`; the originals are never modified.

## 1. 3D / virtual tour

| Source | What it actually is | Result | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Adinarayan_3D_File.exe` (389 MB), identical to `3D_Walkthrough/Milan Park Dombivli_Windows (1).exe` | A **3DVista standalone tour launcher** (Electron). It is *not* a Unity/Unreal build and contains **no building 3D model**. At byte 1,805,681 it holds a zstd-compressed tar (609 MB uncompressed) of the Electron runtime plus `resources/app/`, which is the complete 3DVista web export. | `scripts/extract-tour.py` recovers the full web export into `public/virtual-tour/`: ~23,000 files, ~320 MB, **27 panoramas**, including `script.js`, `script_general.js` and `script_mobile.js` | `/experience` (full-screen iframe, loaded only on click) |
| `milan_web_extract/` (the ChatGPT recovery) | A **partial** carve of the same data: 5 of 27 panoramas, no `script.js`, and one panorama truncated. | Superseded — not used | — |

What the tour contains (from its locale data, verified in the browser):
- Aerial View, Window View and Renders
- **Floor-wise view: 1st to 23rd floor** (real site photos from each floor height)
- Day / Evening / Night
- Connectivity: Education, Healthcare, Shopping & Entertainment, Garden, Temple
- A Google Maps pin at 19.203104, 73.08951

Seven panoramas have no "back" cube face. That is how they were authored (partial spheres), not data loss: the archive's last member, `vulkan-1.dll`, confirms the tar stream was read to the end.

The tour's title was "Milan Park Dombivli" (the plot's society name). It is rebranded to RADIANCE in `index.htm` and `locale/en.txt`. The tour's skin images already carry the Adinarayan logo.

The **`.glb` files** inside the tour (`lib/webxr-input-profiles/`) are VR-controller models, not the building.
